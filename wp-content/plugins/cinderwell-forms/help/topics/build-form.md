Create a form under **Forms → Add New**. Add fields in the order visitors should complete them, configure any notifications and the confirmation, then publish the form.

Add the **Form** block to a page and select the published form. Submissions appear under **Forms → Submissions**. Submitted answers are read-only; you can mark submissions read, star them, export them, or delete them.

For an email signup, choose **Add newsletter form** from the Forms screen. The starter includes a required email field, a newsletter confirmation, and a Subscribe button. Its form type automatically uses the compact newsletter presentation. You can also insert the **Newsletter Form** variation or change an existing Form block's Presentation setting without changing the stored form.

Conditional fields still have a usable non-JavaScript version. Use short, direct labels and instructions, and do not rely on placeholder text in place of a label.
