# Search appearance

Select the **Search appearance** icon in the editor toolbar to open the dedicated SEO sidebar. It previews and adjusts how public content can appear in search results and social sharing without crowding the Post or Block settings.

## SEO readiness

The score is an editing checklist, not a prediction of search ranking. Expand **Review checks** to see what passes and what can be improved. Nothing in the panel prevents publishing.

The focus phrase helps the checklist understand the page topic. It is not published as a meta keyword.

## Search visibility

The content list reports whether local settings make an item **Indexable**, **Noindex**, **Canonical elsewhere**, or unavailable to search engines. This does not confirm whether Google or another search engine has indexed the URL.

When Members Portal protects an entire page, or the page is used for login,
registration, or member profiles, the Portal owns the privacy setting. SEO
shows the locked status, removes the page from scoring, and disables metadata
controls that could expose private content. A public page with a Member Only
block remains eligible for search because only part of that page is gated.

## Inherited values

Leave a field blank to inherit the WordPress title, excerpt, content summary, featured image, or site default. Use the advanced controls only when a page needs a different canonical URL or indexing instruction.
