Use the **Video** block for a video visitors intentionally watch. Choose an uploaded file or paste a supported provider URL, select an aspect ratio, and add a poster image and WebVTT captions when appropriate.

Hero and Section blocks can use video from their **Background** panel. Open **Video settings** to replace the clip, choose its crop and focal position, add a poster, or adjust the overlay. Background video is muted, loops automatically, includes a visitor pause control, and respects reduced-motion preferences.
