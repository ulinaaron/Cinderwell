Client themes can add, replace, or remove fields through `cinderwell_company_details_fields`, `cinderwell_location_fields`, and `cinderwell_portfolio_fields`.

Use `cinderwell_loop_query_args` to narrow results, `cinderwell_loop_link_behavior` to control destinations, and `cinderwell_loop_item_html` only when the item markup truly needs a project-specific override.
